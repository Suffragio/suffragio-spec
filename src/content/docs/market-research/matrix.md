A tabular version of the assessments from [Findings](/suffragio-spec/market-research/findings/) — the same 45 systems, the same requirements, the same verdicts. Full justifications and source links for each entry are in the Findings document.

**Legend:** ✅ yes · ❌ no · ⚠️ partial/conditional · ❓ no public information found.

**Column abbreviations** (full requirement names — see [Motivation & Requirements](/suffragio-spec/motivation/)):

- **Cost** — Low-cost administration
- **Univ.** — Universality
- **Formula** — Any electoral formula
- **Verif.** — Verifiability and openness
- **1 vote** — One ballot per voter + revocation
- **Secrecy** — Ballot secrecy
- **Identity** — Identity verification and delegation
- **Indep.** — Digital independence
